# hello, terminal

My old portfolio was fine. It had spotlights, a globe, a bento grid — all the Aceternity greatest hits. It also had nothing on it that was mine.

So I rebuilt it. The new one is a terminal, because:

- it's honest: this is where I spend most of my day.
- it forces constraints: monospace, four colors, no decoration without purpose.
- it leaves room for weirdness: a `/hobbies` directory, a `/photos` roll, a konami code.

> The medium should hint at what the person is like. A dev portfolio that looks like a landing page is kind of a lie.
